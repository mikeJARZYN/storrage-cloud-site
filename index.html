How payment works

  • Move-in dates. Licences start on the 1st or the 16th of a month and always end on the last day of a month. Ask for another date and we'll offer the next 1st or 16th.
  • Before you get access you pay your first month plus your final month in advance. That's two months' rent for a 1st-of-month start.
  • Starting on the 16th? You also pay an initial half-month alignment charge (16th to month-end) of half the monthly rate. It's rent for the half month you occupy, not a fee or deposit, and it brings your licence in line with calendar-month billing. Your licence then runs the full 3, 6 or 12 months from the 1st of the next month.
  • In between, each remaining month is collected automatically on the 1st by card (Stripe) or Direct Debit (GoCardless). Nothing is taken for the final month — it's already paid.
  • Your rate is fixed for the whole licence term. Prices are reviewed once every three years; the current schedule runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2029 and any change is published on this site before it takes effect.

Rates shown are for a 20ft container and apply to business licences only. All prices in GBP. Receipts are issued for every payment for your accounts.
